What is the Illuminate Workshop?

Illuminate is E-Cell IIT Bombay's premier national entrepreneurship workshop series designed to demystify startup building, venture strategy, business model architecture, and fundraising mechanics for ambitious engineering students. Hosted directly on-campus at Raghu Engineering College, it brings real-world founder frameworks, live pitch breakdowns, and nationwide credentials directly to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to participate in Illuminate 2026?

All undergraduate college students from any engineering branch (CSE, CSM, CSC, EEE, Civil, Mech, IoT, etc.) across 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th years are eligible. No prior business or startup experience is required.

Till when is the Early Bird pass available?

The Early Bird pass (₹699 instead of ₹799) is available strictly until September 30, 2026, at 10:00 PM IST. After 10:00 PM on September 30, passes will revert to the regular price of ₹799. Reserve your seat early to lock in the special pricing.

How will my payment and seat registration be verified?

Once you transfer ₹699 via the QR code or direct UPI button and submit your 12-digit UTR along with the screenshot proof, our operations desk validates the transaction ledger and issues your verified confirmation email.
